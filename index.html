A few house rules we try to live by.

  • 1Give wildlife room. If it changes what it's doing, you're too close.
  • 2Slow down on the water. Manatees can't move out of the way fast.
  • 3Never feed wild animals, gators included.
  • 4Leave every spot better than you found it.
